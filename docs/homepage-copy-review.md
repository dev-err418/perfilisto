# Homepage copy review

The audience wants a professional photo they feel comfortable using on LinkedIn, a CV, or a company profile. The primary action remains “Create my headshots.” Tone: professional, friendly, and direct.

These are editorial candidates, not measured conversion results. The selected copy combines personal confidence with concrete reasons to act: starting price, little effort, and a useful gallery. No promises of jobs, promotions, or better response rates.

| Section | Confidence direction | Convenience direction | Value direction | Selected |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | Professional headshots. A photo you’re proud to share. | Professional headshots. Without the photoshoot. | Professional headshots from 29€. | Confidence; subtitle explains the product, price, and convenience. |
| How it works | A profile photo you feel good about. | Refresh your profile in as little as 15 minutes. | Your next headshot starts with photos you already have. | Convenience; subtitle specifies Executive and when delivery starts. |
| Results | Everyday photos. A professional first impression. | From your camera roll to your work profile. | See what your photos can become. | Confidence, supported immediately by the comparisons. |
| Comparison | A professional look, on your terms. | Upgrade your photo. Keep your day. | A new headshot without a studio booking. | Convenience; makes saved time tangible. |
| Pricing | Find a photo you’re proud to use. | Choose your gallery. Pay once. | One purchase. A fresh look everywhere. | Value; connects the purchase to several real uses. |
| FAQ | Feel confident before you start. | Your questions, answered. | Know what you’re getting. | Confidence; reduces uncertainty before purchase. |

## Four steps: candidates and selection

| Step | Action-led | Benefit-led (selected) | Alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Choose your outfits and backgrounds | Find a look that feels like you | Dress for the way you work |
| 2 | Upload 6–10 photos | Skip the photoshoot | Start with photos you already have |
| 3 | Generate your headshots | Get more looks to choose from | Get on with your day |
| 4 | Download your favorites | Share your next first impression | Put your best photo to work |

Each step’s body keeps the practical instruction, so benefit-led titles do not make the process harder to follow. The upload step still explains the photo requirements; the generation step explains confirmation and the email; the last step describes choosing and downloading.

## CTA candidates

- **Create my headshots** (retained): clear action and personal outcome.
- Get my professional headshots: more explicit, but longer.
- Upgrade my profile photo: benefit-focused, but less explicit about creating a gallery.

## Suggested first experiment

Compare the selected hero against “Professional headshots. Without the photoshoot.” Keep the rest of the page identical. Measure completed purchases per unique eligible landing visitor, with checkout starts as a secondary measure. Do not call either variant a winner based on clicks alone. Tracking needs to be verified before running the experiment.
